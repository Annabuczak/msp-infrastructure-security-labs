# Shared-mailbox folders after moving to New Outlook

[All tickets](../README.md#tickets)

**Date:** 19 November 2026  
**Systems:** Classic Outlook, New Outlook, Exchange Online, Atera  
**Status:** Visibility improved; full user validation pending

## Issue

A user reported that some folders visible in Classic Outlook appeared to be missing after switching to New Outlook.

## What I did

1. I reviewed screenshots of the Classic Outlook navigation and its long Favourites list.
2. I connected to the managed laptop through Atera and inspected the New Outlook folder pane.
3. I located the primary and shared mailboxes and expanded their Inbox hierarchies.
4. I confirmed that multiple folders were present in those hierarchies.
5. I observed that Favourites contained only a subset of the previous shortcuts and distinguished those shortcuts from the actual mailbox folders.
6. I discussed the available shared-mailbox conversion option, but completion of that change was not established in the notes.

## Findings

The inspected folders existed in New Outlook; their visibility differed from the familiar Classic Outlook shortcuts. I found no evidence of deletion during this investigation. A complete comparison of every reported folder was not recorded.

## Outcome

Multiple primary and shared-mailbox folders were located and made visible in the navigation. Full user acceptance and a one-to-one reconciliation remained outstanding.

## Follow-up

Compare the exact outstanding folder names and confirm their hosting mailbox. If necessary, compare the shared mailbox in Outlook on the web before changing permissions or creating folders.

## Skills used

Remote support; Outlook migration troubleshooting; shared-mailbox hierarchy; avoiding unnecessary changes.
