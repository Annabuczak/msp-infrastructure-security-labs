# Scoping an 8x8 telephone-number change

[All tickets](../README.md#tickets)

**Date:** 9 November 2026  
**Systems:** 8x8 Admin Console, cloud telephony  
**Status:** Requirements awaiting confirmation

## Issue

A request was raised to change a telephone number in 8x8, but the exact setting to change had not been established.

## What I did

1. I identified that the request could mean a direct-dial assignment, outbound caller ID, forwarding or the main business number.
2. I reviewed the relevant administration approach for a user-assigned number change.
3. I held off on a configuration change until the intended setting and impact were clear.

## Findings

The immediate blocker was incomplete requirements, rather than a diagnosed platform fault. The notes record guidance and scoping, not a completed number change.

## Outcome

No configuration change or test completion was recorded.

## Follow-up

Confirm the requested setting, authorisation and number availability. Record the current configuration, apply the approved change and test the relevant inbound and outbound behaviour.

## Skills used

Telephony request scoping; change planning; avoiding changes based on ambiguous requirements.
