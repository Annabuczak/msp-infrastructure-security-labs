# External access to a Teams recording

[All tickets](../README.md#tickets)

**Date:** Date not recorded  
**Systems:** Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive  
**Status:** Blocked by hosting-content access

## Issue

An external user needed access to a Teams recording. I did not have access to the SharePoint content hosting it.

## What I did

1. I reviewed how to locate the recording from the meeting chat or recap.
2. I considered the recording’s sharing interface and specific-people access.
3. I identified my lack of access to the hosting SharePoint content as the blocker to completing the change.

## Findings

The investigation needed the recording owner or an authorised site administrator. A link alone would not resolve the underlying permissions issue.

## Outcome

The access limitation was identified and raised. Successful external sharing was not confirmed.

## Follow-up

Confirm the storage location and owner, check the organisation’s external-sharing policy and recording sensitivity, and ask the authorised owner to grant permitted access to the intended recipient. Test that access with the recipient.

## Skills used

Teams and SharePoint support; permissions troubleshooting; external-sharing scoping.
